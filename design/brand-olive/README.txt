INTO CANVA PRO
1. Brand Kit → Logos → upload mypromo-mark.svg, mypromo-mark-white.svg,
   mypromo-lockup.png.
2. Brand Kit → Colours → New palette → the eleven hexes from the palette file.
3. Brand Kit → Fonts → Tajawal (free, Google Fonts, SIL OFL). Upload it if
   Canva's list has not got it.

THE THREE RULES
1. Olive is the brand AND the action, so there is only ever one olive button
   on a screen. Everything else is ink on ivory.
2. Clay means "this needs you" — never decoration.
3. Light only. Warm white cards on ivory, one radius, one hairline.

MyPromo — زيتون
The palette, as chosen 20/09/2026. Everything below is a flat hex, so it can
be typed straight into Canva's Brand Kit or into app/globals.css.

THE BRAND
#2A5B3E   Olive        the brand AND the action. Buttons, the chosen tab,
                       the live dot, the second figure of the mark.
#3F7A57   Olive light  pressed and hover states.
#E3EDE5   Olive pale   chips, the tint behind an icon, a soft highlight.

ATTENTION  — used sparingly, and never as decoration.
#A8502A   Clay         the one thing that needs you: a warning, a duel
                       waiting, an unanswered question.
#F2E3DA   Clay pale    the ground under a warning.

GROUND
#F3F1E9   Background   the ivory field everything sits on.
#FFFDF8   Surface      every card — warm white, not pure white.

THE RULES
1. Olive is both the brand and the action — so there is only ever ONE olive
   button on a screen. Everything else is ink on ivory.
2. Clay means "this needs you". Never decoration.
3. Light only. Warm white cards on ivory, one radius, one hairline.
4. The mark is never redrawn, recoloured or stretched. Clear space of one
   head's width on every side.

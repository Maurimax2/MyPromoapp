MyPromo — brand export for Canva
=================================
Everything here comes out of the app itself: the mark from app/icon.svg, the
colours from the tokens in app/globals.css that every screen reads. Nothing was
redrawn or re-picked by eye, so what you design in Canva and what a student
sees on their phone are the same colours.

WHAT IS IN HERE
---------------
logo/
  mypromo-mark.svg              the mark, vector. Upload THIS to Canva first —
                                it stays sharp at any size, from a favicon to
                                a banner.
  mypromo-mark-white.svg        all white, for the purple header or a photo.
  mypromo-mark-ink.svg          one ink (#1A1424), for a stamp or a print with
                                one colour.
  mypromo-icon-rounded.svg      the mark inset in a rounded square — app icon,
                                profile picture, WhatsApp group photo.
  mypromo-mark-512/1024/2048.png   transparent PNG, if Canva refuses an SVG.
  mypromo-mark-white-1024.png      transparent, white.
  mypromo-mark-ink-1024.png        transparent, one ink.
  mypromo-icon-1024.png            the rounded icon.
  mypromo-lockup.png            mark + "MyPromo" side by side, transparent.
  mypromo-lockup-white.png      the same, for a dark or purple ground.

palette/
  mypromo-colours.txt           every hex with what it is for, and the twelve
                                to paste into Canva's Brand Kit in order.

mypromo-brand-sheet.png         one page: the mark on four grounds, the whole
                                palette, the typeface, and the three rules.

PUTTING IT INTO CANVA PRO
-------------------------
1. Brand Kit → Logos → upload mypromo-mark.svg, mypromo-mark-white.svg and
   mypromo-lockup.png.
2. Brand Kit → Colours → New palette, then add the twelve hexes from
   palette/mypromo-colours.txt in that order. Canva takes them typed with the
   # sign.
3. Brand Kit → Fonts → IBM Plex Sans Arabic. If Canva's font list has not got
   it, download it free from fonts.google.com/specimen/IBM+Plex+Sans+Arabic
   and upload it — the licence (SIL Open Font) allows that.

THE THREE RULES, WHICH ARE THE WHOLE IDENTITY
---------------------------------------------
1. ORANGE IS ATTENTION, NEVER DECORATION. One action on a screen, and the
   thing that needs you. A poster where orange is just a nice colour reads as
   a different app.
2. THE MARK IS NEVER REDRAWN. No recolouring, no stretching, no shadow behind
   it, no rotation. Keep clear space of one head's width on every side.
3. LIGHT ONLY. White cards on #F1F5F9, one radius, one shadow. There is no
   dark version of this identity yet — if you make one in Canva it will not
   match the app.

AND ONE MORE, FOR ANYTHING WITH WORDS ON IT
-------------------------------------------
The interface language is Arabic. Every piece of study content — module names,
lecture titles, QCM, anatomy — stays French. Never write a medical or
anatomical term in Arabic. S1 and S2 are never translated.

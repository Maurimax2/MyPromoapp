MyPromo — the palette
Every one of these is taken from app/globals.css, where the app itself reads them.

BRAND
#6B21B5   Purple            the brand. Headers, primary buttons, the chosen thing.
#8B5CF6   Purple light      hover, focus rings, the lighter half of the logo.
#EDE9FE   Purple pale       chips, tags, the tint behind an icon.

ATTENTION  — used sparingly. Orange means "this needs you", never decoration.
#F97316   Orange            the one action on a screen. The + button.
#FDBA74   Orange light      the lighter half of the logo's right figure.
#FFF1E3   Orange pale       a warning's ground.

GROUND
#F1F5F9   Background        the field everything sits on.
#FFFFFF   Surface           every card.

INK
#1A1424   Ink               body text, headings.
#453D57   Ink 2             secondary text.
#6B6480   Ink 3             captions, hints, disabled.

LINES
#E8E4F0   Line              borders.
#F1EEF7   Line soft         a divider inside a card.

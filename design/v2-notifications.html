The rules behind them

These are what stop notifications from feeling like spam.

Never at night

Nothing about studying between 22:30 and 08:00 in Nouakchott, whatever triggered it. That ends the 3 AM pushes for good.

At each student's own hour

The app learns when each student usually studies and sends their study push about 30 minutes before. 17:00 until it knows.

Two study pushes a day, at most

Questions, reviews, lectures and the streak share that budget. Messages, challenges and classmates come as they happen, with their own cap.

Always about something real

A real question, lecture, classmate or number. If there is nothing real to say, nothing is sent.

Never the same sentence twice

Each kind has several versions, and a student never sees the same one twice in two weeks.

Backs off when ignored

After three ignored reminders in a row, every other day; after a week, once a week with what's new.

Every kind, with when and why

“Today” means it exists in the app now with fixed wording; “New” means it's new. All of it runs on the server, so none of it needs an app update.

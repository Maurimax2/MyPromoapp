`fra.traineddata.gz` is the French model from Tesseract's `tessdata_fast`,
Apache License 2.0 — https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_fast

It is committed rather than fetched because npm publishes only the 6 MB and
0.7 MB models, and on their photographed papers this one reads 19 questions
out of 20 where those read 16 and 17.
